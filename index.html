What this project explores

Three building blocks of an introductory digital advertising project.

01 / PLAN

Audience & keywords

Understand how search terms and audience targeting connect people with relevant information.

02 / CREATE

Ads & landing pages

Explore clear messages, helpful website content, and the experience after an ad click.

03 / MEASURE

Campaign performance

Learn what impressions, clicks, click-through rate, and conversions mean in advertising reports.

About this website

This is an educational practice project for a digital marketing course. It is not an online store, does not sell products or services, and does not collect visitor information through forms.

Website content is for demonstration and learning purposes only.
